# Legal and Compliance Policy

Document ID: LEG-001
Version: 1.0
Effective: January 1, 2026

## Contract Review
Employees must send customer, vendor, partnership, and non-disclosure agreements to Legal before signing. Only authorized signatories may execute contracts on behalf of the company.

## Records and Privacy
Business records must be retained for 7 years unless Legal issues a different instruction. Personal data may be accessed only for a legitimate business purpose and must not be exported to personal storage.

## Escalation
Potential regulatory notices, litigation threats, or data protection requests must be forwarded to legal@techcorp.in on the same business day. Employees must preserve related records and must not respond independently.

## Confidentiality
Non-disclosure agreements must be completed before sharing confidential information with an external party. Employees must use the current Legal template and must not promise terms that are not approved in writing.

## Gifts and Conflicts
Employees must disclose a potential conflict of interest to Legal and their manager before participating in a related decision. Cash gifts from vendors are prohibited; non-cash gifts above INR 5,000 require Legal review.

## Marketing Claims
Public claims about products, security, performance, or regulatory compliance require Legal and Product approval before publication. Evidence supporting an approved claim must be retained with the campaign record.
