# Finance and Expense Policy

Document ID: FIN-001
Version: 1.0
Effective: January 1, 2026

## Travel Reimbursement
Employees may claim economy airfare or rail travel and reasonable hotel costs for approved business travel. The domestic meal allowance is INR 1,200 per day. Claims must be submitted in the expense system within 30 calendar days after returning.

## Approval Rules
Expenses up to INR 25,000 require manager approval. Expenses above INR 25,000 require manager and Finance approval before purchase. Unapproved personal expenses are not reimbursable.

## Equipment and Software
Business software purchases require manager approval and a Finance cost-center code. Receipts must be attached to every reimbursement claim. Finance reviews claims within 10 business days.

## Expense Category Codes
Every reimbursement claim must be tagged with one of the following category codes in the expense system:
- EXP-TVL-01: Domestic travel and lodging
- EXP-TVL-02: International travel
- EXP-ENT-05: Client entertainment (business meals with external clients)
- EXP-TEL-03: Telecom and internet bills (mobile/broadband reimbursement)
- EXP-OFC-08: Office supplies and books

Claims submitted without a valid expense category code are automatically rejected by the expense system.

## Corporate Cards
Corporate cards may be used only for approved business expenses. Cardholders must reconcile transactions with receipts within 10 calendar days of the statement date. Personal or unsupported transactions must be repaid to the company within 15 days.

## Foreign Currency
Foreign expenses are converted using the card settlement amount or the published company exchange rate for the transaction date. Bank charges may be claimed when shown on the receipt. Cash withdrawals are not reimbursable unless Finance approved them in advance.

## Exceptions and Audit
Any exception requires written approval from the Finance Controller before the expense is incurred. Finance may request supporting documents for audit, and incomplete or duplicate claims may be rejected.
