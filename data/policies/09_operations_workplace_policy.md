# Operations and Workplace Policy

Document ID: OPS-001
Version: 1.0
Effective: January 1, 2026

## Office Access
Employees must display their access badge while on company premises and must not allow another person to enter through a secured door using their badge. Visitors require host registration and escort.

## Business Continuity
During a critical operational incident, employees must follow the incident commander instructions and use the approved status channel. Department heads review continuity plans every quarter.

## Procurement Requests
Operational supply requests must be submitted through the procurement portal at least 5 business days before the required delivery date. Emergency purchases require department head approval and a written justification.

## Health and Safety
Hazards, injuries, and unsafe conditions must be reported to Workplace Operations immediately and recorded before the end of the workday. Employees must follow evacuation instructions and must not re-enter an evacuated area until cleared.

## Facilities Requests
Facilities requests for repairs, seating, or access badges must be submitted through the workplace service desk. Emergency facilities issues may be reported to the duty coordinator by phone and must be logged afterward.

## Supplier Delivery
Deliveries must be received at the designated loading point and checked against the purchase order. Damaged or incomplete deliveries must be recorded with photographs and reported to Procurement within 2 business days.
